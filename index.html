A swipe from the watch face.

Four Wear OS tiles put your steps, your leaderboard rank, your shoe and your crates on your wrist. They update from your phone, even with the app closed.

StepsYour steps climbing toward your goal, how far is left, and your distance and calories.
  • Climbing to your goal
  • The last tenth, and every step past it
  • Goal hit
Leaderboard RankWhere you stand today and how long is left to climb, plus your week, month and streak.
  • Hours left on today's board
  • The last 6 hours
  • A podium place: gold, silver or bronze
Gear StatusIts XP and damage side by side, its level, and what any damage is costing you.
  • XP to the next level
  • Damage past 70%: it starts to cost you
  • From 85%: your rank is at risk
CratesThe crates you earned today, how close the next one is, and when each one unlocks.
  • Everyday crates, red once the day is full
  • Spots only a pass can open
  • Your next crate, building

The watch app installs from Google Play on the watch, and WALKn on your phone shows when the two are linked.
