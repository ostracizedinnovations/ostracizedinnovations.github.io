Your privacy choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a US state with a privacy law, WALKn asks for your choices about ads and analytics the first time you open the app.

You can change that answer at any time in the app: Profile → Support → Ad privacy settings.

If you decline, ads cannot be loaded — so the optional "watch an ad" rewards stop working. Nothing else changes: step tracking, your progress, leaderboards and every other feature work exactly the same either way.

You can delete everything we hold at any time via Profile → Account → Delete Account.

What we collect

📍

Location & movement

GPS coordinates are used while your shoe is active to verify walking displacement and rule out vehicle movement. Raw coordinates are never stored on our servers or shared with third parties — only your displacement distance is retained.

📱

Sensor data

Accelerometer readings validate your walking gait as part of our anti-cheat system. This data is processed on-device in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

👟

Game progress & activity

Step counts, distance, estimated calories, daily history, streaks, goals, shoes, inventory, and Foot Prints currency are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal Firebase account for backup and cross-device restore.

If you fill in the optional calorie profile, the height, weight, sex and age you enter are stored with your settings and used only to make the calorie estimate more accurate.

🏆

Leaderboards (optional)

If you choose to link a Google account, we receive your display name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In. Linking is optional and only required for leaderboard participation.

When you compete, the following becomes readable by other signed-in players: your display name and profile photo, your step counts for the current period, your streak length, your equipped shoe's appearance and condition, and any achievements you have chosen to feature. Your email address is never shown to other players.

📢

Ads

WALKn shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and usage signals in order to serve and measure ads, and to limit how often you see the same one.

Where consent is required, we ask before any ad is requested, and we do not request one until you have answered. If you allow it, ads may be personalised using the data Google holds. If you decline, no ad is requested at all.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

📊

Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand whether the app actually works after you install it — for example, whether you granted the location permission, and whether step and distance tracking ever started. These are counts of in-app events, not a record of your activity. Analytics also collects a randomly generated app-instance identifier, basic device information (model and OS version), and an approximate region derived from your IP address. We do not send your name, email address, or GPS coordinates to Analytics.

Where consent is required, analytics collection stays switched off unless you allow it.

🛠️

Crash & error reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to record crashes and handled errors so they can be fixed. A report contains the error and its stack trace, your device model and OS version, and a randomly generated installation identifier. It contains no location data, no account details and nothing you have typed.

We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working for this, so it is not covered by the consent choice above. If you would prefer we did not collect crash reports from your device, email us and we will tell you how to opt out.

💬

Feedback

If you submit a bug report or suggestion through the app, we store the type you selected, the description you wrote, your user ID, and the time you sent it. We use this only to respond to and improve the app. If you instead use the "email a screenshot" option, the message goes to our email inbox rather than our database.

What we never collect

  • Your contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone or camera.
  • Raw GPS coordinates on our servers — only the distance you covered.
  • Any medical or health-record information. WALKn measures activity (steps, distance, an estimated calorie figure); it is not a medical device and makes no health assessment.
  • Payment details. WALKn has no purchases, so we never see a card.

We do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles ourselves.

How we use your data

  • To count and verify your steps during walks
  • To detect cheating so leaderboards stay fair
  • To maintain your game progress across devices
  • To power competitive leaderboards
  • To deliver push notifications (level-up completions, crate alerts, streak reminders, shoe wear warnings)
  • To serve optional rewarded ads via Google AdMob
  • To measure whether core features — step tracking, distance measurement, and rewards — are working after install
  • To diagnose crashes and errors
  • To read and respond to your feedback

Because WALKn runs on Google's infrastructure, your data may be stored on or accessed from servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google provides the safeguards for these transfers — including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses — as described in its own privacy documentation.

Data retention

Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or delete your account. Cloud-synced data — including your game state, daily history, leaderboard entries, player card and settings — is permanently deleted when you use Profile → Account → Delete Account. This action cannot be undone and there is no recovery period.

Feedback you send us is kept for as long as it is useful for fixing the issue you reported, and no longer than two years. Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months.

If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law

Depending on your state — including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah and Virginia — you may have the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to request a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to opt out of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of those rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we never have. Allowing personalised ads may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, or as "targeted advertising", under some of these laws — declining the ads choice, or changing it later in Profile → Support → Ad privacy settings, opts you out. You can also delete everything via Profile → Account → Delete Account, or email us and we will handle any of the above.
