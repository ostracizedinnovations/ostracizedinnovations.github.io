Privacy Policy
Effective date: July 28, 2026  ·  Developer: Ostracized Innovations

What we collect

📍

Location & movement

GPS coordinates are used while your shoe is active to verify walking displacement and rule out vehicle movement. Raw coordinates are never stored on our servers or shared with third parties — only your displacement distance is retained.

📱

Sensor data

Accelerometer readings validate your walking gait as part of our anti-cheat system. This data is processed on-device in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

👟

Game progress

Step counts, daily history, streaks, goals, shoes, inventory, and Foot Prints currency are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal Firebase account for backup and cross-device restore.

🏆

Leaderboards (optional)

If you choose to link a Google account, we receive your display name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In. Linking is optional and only required for leaderboard participation. Your display name, step count, streak length, and equipped shoe appearance are visible to other players when you compete.

📢

Ads

WALKn uses Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage signals. See Google's privacy policy for details.

📊

Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand whether the app actually works after you install it — for example, whether you granted the location permission, and whether step and distance tracking ever started. These are counts of in-app events, not a record of your activity. Analytics also collects a randomly generated app-instance identifier, basic device information (model and OS version), and an approximate region derived from your IP address. We do not send your name, email address, or GPS coordinates to Analytics.

🛠️

Crash & error reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to record crashes and handled errors so they can be fixed. A report contains the error and its stack trace, your device model and OS version, and a randomly generated installation identifier.

💬

Feedback

If you submit a bug report or suggestion through the app, the submission text and your user ID are stored in our database. We use this only to respond to and improve the app.

How we use your data

  • To count and verify your steps during walks
  • To maintain your game progress across devices
  • To power competitive leaderboards
  • To deliver push notifications (level-up completions, crate alerts, streak reminders, shoe wear warnings)
  • To serve ads via Google AdMob
  • To measure whether core features — step tracking, distance measurement, and rewards — are working after install
  • To diagnose crashes and errors
  • To read and respond to your feedback

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking beyond what Google AdMob does to serve ads.

Third-party services

WALKn relies on the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, cloud data backup, and Firestore database.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional account linking for leaderboard participation.
  • Google AdMob — Advertising.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Usage analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Crash and error reporting.

All are Google services: policies.google.com/privacy

Data retention

Local data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or delete your account. Cloud-synced data — including your game state, leaderboard entries, and settings — is permanently deleted when you use Profile → Account → Delete Account. This action cannot be undone.

Your rights

  • Delete your data — Remove all cloud data at any time via Profile → Account → Delete Account.
  • Revoke Google access — Disconnect Google Sign-In from your Google account's connected apps settings at any time.
  • Contact us — Email us at ostracizedinnovations@gmail.com with any data-related request.

Children's privacy

WALKn is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
